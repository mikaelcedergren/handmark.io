Why Handmark exists

The human part is becoming harder to see.

People value the effort behind a thing. A ring, an article, a chair, or a painting carries more meaning when another human being has spent attention on it and stood behind the result.

Handmark exists for the work where that answer matters. It gives honest makers a way to make the human role visible without asking buyers to simply take their word for it.

The standard

Selective by design.

Tools can support the work. Handmark is about whether a person is still the author: making the judgment calls, carrying responsibility, and being able to explain the process.

01

Walkthrough required

Submitted proof is the starting point. We contact the applicant and ask them to walk us through the process, digitally or in person.

02

Evidence required

Applicants support the claim through process material, references, studio context, work-in-progress records, direct contact, or inspection.

03

No proof, no stamp

The mark is only issued when the claim is satisfactory. The stamp is not a decoration, and it is not granted on trust alone.

Membership

There are no tiers and no lightweight version of approval. The review starts at $99 and covers the human review plus walkthrough. If approved, membership continues at $79 per month. If not approved, no subscription starts.
